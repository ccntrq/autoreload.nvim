## v1.1.0 - 2026-06-24
### Added
* Configurable conflict handling via the `conflict` option, including a blocking
`"prompt"` strategy that lets you reload, keep, or diff against disk when an
external change collides with unsaved edits. #2

Suggested by @mightyiam in #1
### Deprecated
* `notify.on_conflict` is superseded by `conflict.strategy` (still honored as a
legacy alias: `true` → `"notify"`, `false` → `"none"`). #2
